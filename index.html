HOW IT WORKS

From a simple signal to meaningful communication.

ASHA helps turn supported inputs into confirmed requests that caregivers can understand and respond to.

  1. 01

    Choose Your Way

    Use touch, calibrated gestures, or assisted speech.

  2. 02

    Express a Need

    ASHA interprets the selected input into a proposed message.

  3. 03

    Confirm the Message

    Review and explicitly confirm a consequential request.

  4. 04

    Stay Connected

    A caregiver receives the request and can acknowledge it.
